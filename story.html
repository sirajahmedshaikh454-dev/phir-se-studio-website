Why we exist

Textile is Pakistan's biggest export. Its scraps go nowhere.

A study reported by Dawn in 2025 puts Pakistan's textile waste at around 270,000 tonnes a year. Much of it is clean, bright, usable cloth from the cutting rooms of export factories.

At the same time, skilled stitchers, especially women working near home, often struggle to find steady, fairly paid work.

Phir Se Studio connects those two facts: scraps become raw material, and the raw material becomes wages.

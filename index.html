01

We collect the offcuts

Clean trimmings from export units around Faisalabad, picked up by weight and sorted before they ever reach a landfill.

02

Sort, wash, colour-match

Every scrap is washed and grouped by colour and fibre, so patches age and fade together.

03

Plan the patchwork

Our designer-cutter lays out each piece on the table by hand. This is where the ralli rhythm is born.

04

Stitch, line by line

Trained local women piece and quilt every item, paid at or above the Punjab minimum wage.

05

Tag, check, ship

A QR tag records the fabric saved and who made it. Then it travels to your home.
